# Next Step A: AI-Powered Stem Mastering & Dynamic EQ Analyzer — Proposal

## Context
OpenBand provides stem separation and mastering suites, but mastering is currently applied to the master mixdown rather than individual stems dynamically.

## Objectives
- Introduce automated stem frequency analysis to detect masking between vocals, drums, bass, and melody.
- Provide AI-driven EQ notch and dynamic compression suggestions per stem.
- Add unit tests for AI mix analysis.
