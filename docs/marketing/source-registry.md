# Marketing Source Registry

> Purpose: keep external evidence reusable, dated, scoped, and auditable. This registry records what a source can support and, equally important, what it cannot support.

## Rules

- Prefer first-party product documentation, official company announcements, recognized industry bodies, app stores, and primary legal/public records.
- A source supports only the proposition actually evidenced by it. Do not use a large creator-platform user count as a direct TAM estimate for OpenBand.
- Pricing, plans, product features, app-store availability, and policy pages are volatile. Re-check before external use.
- Promotional pages may show campaign-specific prices that differ from canonical plan documentation.
- Market reports about recorded-music revenue describe the music economy; they do not automatically size music-production software demand.
- When sources conflict, preserve both observations and resolve which surface is authoritative for the specific claim.

## Source quality

| Grade | Meaning | Typical use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A | Primary/official source with direct authority over the claim | product behavior, pricing, industry toplines, app-store listing |
| B | Primary company statement with promotional or methodological caveats | user/community scale, company positioning, campaign offers |
| C | Reputable secondary analysis with disclosed method | triangulation only |
| D | Weak/opaque secondary estimate | discovery lead; do not use as decision evidence without corroboration |

## Freshness defaults

| Claim type | Default re-check window |
| --- | --- |
| Price / subscription plan | 30 days and immediately before publication |
| Product feature / plan entitlement | 60 days |
| App-store availability / naming collision | 30 days |
| Company audience/community scale | 90 days |
| Platform policy / discovery guidance | 90 days and before a major launch |
| Annual industry report | when next annual edition is published |
| Legal/trademark/domain availability | at decision time; never rely on an old snapshot |

## Conflict handling

When two official surfaces disagree:

1. record both sources;
2. identify whether one is promotional, regional, stale, or plan-specific;
3. use the narrower claim;
4. re-check the live purchase/plan surface immediately before publishing price-sensitive copy;
5. never silently choose the number that makes OpenBand look better.
