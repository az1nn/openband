## FAQ

### Why another browser music studio?

The initial wedge is immediate creation plus local-first project ownership and inspectable source. The goal is a trustworthy core loop, not the longest feature list.

### Do I need an account?

The launch contract includes a visitor path for local projects. State the tested release and any workflow that does require an account or hosted service.

### Is it ready for important production work?

It is an alpha. Publish supported browsers and known limitations, and advise creators to keep independent backups until persistence and recovery have release evidence.

### Is everything free?

Core creation is intended to remain free. Optional hosted sync, collaboration, or compute may have separate economics later; no public pricing promise exists until product policy and implementation agree.

### Where does project data go?

Answer per workflow. Local project creation, account sync, collaboration, stem processing, and external AI providers can have different data paths. Never compress them into a blanket privacy claim.

### Who owns music made with it?

Do not replace legal terms with marketing copy. Explain project storage and export behavior, then note that samples, performances, models, and third-party services retain their applicable terms.
